# Storyboard

## Video Direction

The AI Travel Advisor is the hero. The spot moves directly from travel intent into the real `Buscador inteligente` surface, watches one natural-language request become a loading state, and resolves into destination recommendations without leaving the product. Motion is restrained, directional and synchronized to the three story transitions.

## Frame 01: Idea (0-1.5s)

- Visual: full-bleed tropical coast image with a dark cinematic grade.
- Motion: slow push-in, short copy reveal and blue rule draw.
- Text: "Tu próximo viaje empieza con una idea."

## Frame 02: AI Advisor Interaction (1.5-5.5s)

- Visual: recreated real `/search` interface from `search.vue`: title "Buscador inteligente", subtitle explaining users can write what they want, text field, and "Buscar con IA" button.
- Interaction: type "Quiero una escapada de playa para este verano" into the field.
- AI processing: submit the form, switch the real button to its loading state and show the real skeleton-card pattern.
- Motion: one controlled product push-in, a field highlight and a cursor move to the CTA.
- Text: "Describe tu viaje."

## Frame 03: Recommended Results (5.5-8.3s)

- Visual: real result structure from the page: "Recomendación de la IA" card plus destination cards.
- Text: "Tu viaje. A tu medida."
- Results: Cancún, Bali and Phuket, all present in the frontend destination image map, with local frozen imagery and no invented prices.
- Motion: explanation and cards reveal in reading order; the Bali card receives the final emphasis before the match cut.

## Frame 04: Brand (8.3-10s)

- Visual: Bali continues full-bleed behind a high-contrast left-aligned brand lockup.
- Text: "Agencia Viajes", "Descubre. Decide. Viaja." and "Impulsado por IA".
- Motion: match cut from the Bali result and a clean, held final reveal designed to remain legible as a thumbnail.
